Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services, timelines, and coverage.

What areas do you serve?

We operate across the Dhofar Governorate — Salalah, Mirbat, Taqah, and surrounding areas — and take on larger projects throughout Oman and the GCC.

Do you offer free site surveys and quotes?

Yes. For construction, HVAC, and CCTV projects we provide a free on-site assessment and a detailed written quotation with no obligation.

How long does a typical AC installation take?

A single split unit takes 1 day. Multi-zone VRF or central AC systems typically take 1–3 weeks depending on the property size and layout.

Are your products and installations under warranty?

Absolutely. All equipment comes with manufacturer warranty (typically 1–5 years) and our installation work is covered by our own service guarantee.

Do you provide maintenance contracts?

Yes — we offer annual and multi-year preventive maintenance contracts for HVAC, CCTV, and networks, including 24/7 emergency response.

Can you handle combined construction + MEP + security projects?

That's our specialty. AMCTIC is a turnkey contractor — we manage civil work, MEP, HVAC, and security under a single point of contact.
